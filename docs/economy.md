# The economy

Generated by `node tools/economy.js` from the game's own data and payout functions. Rewards for a first clear are paid once
(a claim per mission id); a repeat pays renewable mon (half the first clear's, at least 30) and a quarter of the XP, plus one upgrade
material for a Shadow, an Ink Trial or a mission champion; the Ink Tournament pays every run. Shadow crystals are only ever
paid once. Dailies pay mon each day (not counted below). Durations are **assumptions** for a steady player, not measurements.

## What pays again

Per run, and per hour of doing only that (assumed durations, including the walk to it). The tournament: Every run pays when it ends, for each stage it cleared: losing ends a run and still pays it. Leaving a run, or closing the game during one, forfeits it. Tournaments pay XP, mon and materials, never shadow crystals.

## Does it add up?

Everything above (the supplies aside) costs **19,050 mon** and **36 materials**; the journey pays **7,675 mon** and **10 materials** once.
The gap, **11,375 mon**, is the renewable economy's to close: about **1.8 hours** of The Long Road, 5 stages (full run) (the best mon per hour above), or a mix of repeats and dailies.
Materials: **26** more, from repeats of hard fights and tournament stages (or 300 mon each in the shop).

Nothing here is needed to finish the campaign: every Shadow can be beaten with the starting katana and the powers absorbed on the way. Crystals buy only cosmetics and variants.
