# Moves: frame data, modifier order, input buffer

Generated by `node tools/move-tables.js` from the game itself (the training codex shows the same numbers). Frames are at 60 fps:
*startup* is the frames before the first active frame (a thrown move: before the release), *active* the first to last active frame,
*recovery* the frames after the last active one. Reach is in arena pixels at the fighter's scale; a blow lands when the defender's
centre is within reach + 20 × its scale in front (see `reachOf`; the training hitboxes draw exactly that). Damage is the base, before
the modifiers below.

## The hero, by weapon family

Each family changes the hero's moves (`GEAR.weapons[*].moves`); variants (`class`) share their family's table. Powers are listed in
the codex with their own frame data.

## The order a blow resolves in (`tryHit`)

1. **Reach and height.** In front within `[-40, reachOf]`; on a custom map, within 190 × scale vertically. A high blow misses a crouch, a low blow misses a jump. A throw only catches a fighter on the ground who is free (standing, walking, crouching, guarding) or in block-stun: never one attacking, rolling, reeling, down or in the air.
2. **Invulnerability.** Getting up, after an Issen, the roll's window, a move's own `inv` window (Miststep). Nothing below applies.
3. **The guard.** Blocked if the blow is blockable, the defender faces it, is on the ground, free or in block-stun, and guards the right height (low needs a crouch, high a stand). Guard Assist raises an idle hero's guard against mid and high. In White Mode a guard started within the parry window (0.15 s + the parry modifier) is a **parry** instead. A blocked blow deals only its chip.
4. **Damage, multiplied in this order:** base × rage 1.2 × Demon Sovereignty × the attacker's attack modifier × the chain bonus (the 2nd to 4th cut) × a critical (drawn from the fight's RNG, only when crit > 0) × the weapon traits (first strike, momentum) × the whiff punish (into a recovering move) × White's ward (0.15 unless a punish, a parry, an Issen or an unblockable non-throw) × Iron Skin × Iron Resolve (1 − mitigation) × the defender's (1 − damage mitigation).
5. **After the damage:** burns and bleeding are set; the damage is **counted** (stats, combo, challenges) **before** any healing; Healing Tea heals (and is charged) if the hero fell below its threshold; the Oni-gane Plate turns a first knockdown into a stagger; armour (a move's `armor` window, Iron Skin, Iron Resolve, the ward) keeps a fighter from reeling unless the blow knocks down.

## The input buffer, evaluated

A press is held for **0.28 s (17 frames)** and taken on the first frame the hero is free (`readIn`). A jump press is held 0.15 s. During a chain
cut, Slash after the chain window queues the next cut. Presses made during a roll are dropped (no attacking out of a roll). Pausing, the app
going to the background, a new round and the end of a match clear the buffer and every held key, so nothing stale fires on return.

Measured (a Kick pressed before the first frame the hero can act after a Forward + Slash lunge):

| Pressed before that frame | Kick on that frame? |
|---|---|
| 0.05 s (3 f) | yes, on that frame |
| 0.15 s (9 f) | yes, on that frame |
| 0.25 s (15 f) | yes, on that frame |
| 0.27 s (16 f) | yes, on that frame |
| 0.29 s (17 f) | no (the press expired) |
| 0.35 s (21 f) | no (the press expired) |

Verdict: kept at 0.28 s. A press up to 16 frames (0.27 s) early still lands on the first frame the hero can act, which makes a punish
on a phone reliable. A press made in block-stun (0.2 s) comes out as the guard drops, a counter by design; one made as a 0.3 s reel begins
expires, and one made while knocked down (over a second) always does, so nothing fires late.
